Quick note: most of the room already knows the drill, a few new families are here too, so we'll move fast on the familiar stuff and make sure the new folks are caught up before we break.
  • 1

    Team Philosophy & Goals

    What kind of competitive season this is — development-focused vs. exposure-focused — and what that means for how we run practices and pick tournaments.

  • 2

    Playing Time & Roles

    Everyone gets meaningful innings; tournament lineups are earned in practice. Specific playing-time questions go coach-to-parent privately — never in the stands, never in front of the team.

  • 3

    Communication Protocol

    One channel for team info (app/group chat/email, name it here). 24-hour cooling-off period after tough games before reaching out. Concerns go to the coach directly, not through other parents.

  • 4

    Schedule & Commitment

    Practice days/times, draft tournament calendar, attendance expectations, and how we handle conflicts with school events, other sports, or vacations.

  • 5

    Costs

    Uniforms, tournament fees, travel/hotel estimates, and any fundraising — give a real ballpark for the full season so nothing shows up as a surprise later.

  • 6

    Sideline Conduct

    Parents cheer, coaches coach — no instructing from the fence, no arguing with umpires, no negative talk about other players. It's how the girls hear one coaching voice instead of six.

  • 7

    College Exposure

    Where showcases and recruiting camps fit this season, and what's on the coaching staff versus what's on the player/family to pursue.
