Assumptions
  • Season length estimated at 38 weeks (9–10 months); tournament and practice counts scale across the Low–Mid–High range.
  • Facility rental and coach travel scale with schedule intensity ($4,000–$5,000 and $1,000–$1,500), reflecting more indoor/outdoor time booked in heavier weeks.
  • Coach pay: $25/hr, 10 hrs per tournament, 2 hrs per practice.
  • Fixed costs regardless of schedule: team affiliation ($1,400), team insurance ($500), misc ($1,000).
  • Not included: equipment, uniforms, tournament travel/lodging for families, and umpire fees — add these separately if needed.
